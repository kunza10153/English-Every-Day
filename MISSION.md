# Mission: English for work and everyday international communication

## Why
Use English confidently at work, communicate naturally with international colleagues, and independently understand English videos and texts. This makes work conversations and information access less dependent on translation.

## Success looks like
- Introduce work, ask clarifying questions, and contribute in a routine conversation with an international colleague.
- Follow the main point and important details of an English work video or article.
- Write a clear short work message and read common workplace communication without translation.

## Constraints
- Build speaking, listening, reading, and writing together through short, practical sessions.
- Start with a lightweight baseline check; current level is not yet known.

## Out of scope
- Exam-specific preparation unless it becomes necessary for the work goal.
