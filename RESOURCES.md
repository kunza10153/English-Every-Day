# English for work and communication resources

## Knowledge

- [Council of Europe: CEFR descriptors](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors)
  A practical, researched set of “can do” descriptions for language activities. Use for: setting and checking attainable communication goals.
- [Council of Europe: CEFR self-assessment framework](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/level-descriptions)
  The six proficiency levels and self-assessment grid. Use for: keeping a balanced profile across listening, reading, spoken interaction, and writing.
- [British Council LearnEnglish](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/)
  Practice material organised around real-life English skills. Use for: short listening and reading practice between lessons.
- [Pavlik & Anderson: Vocabulary memory and the spacing effect](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21702785/)
  Experimental research on Japanese–English vocabulary retention. Use for: planning review intervals rather than repeatedly rereading a word.
- [American Psychological Association: Practice for knowledge acquisition](https://www.apa.org/education/k12/practice-acquisition)
  Explains retrieval practice and spaced, frequent testing. Use for: designing short self-checks that reveal durable recall.
